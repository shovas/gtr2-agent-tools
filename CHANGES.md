# Changes

Notes for users of the tools, by day, newest first (generated by `scripts/changelog.py` from the git history and `scripts/changelog_overrides.json`; do not edit by hand). Each day lists highlights first, then improvements, fixes and documentation. The detailed, agent-level changes are in the git history (`git log`).

## 2026-11-16

### Highlights

- The car can reverse out when it gets stuck against a wall and carries on (`243dade`)
- Laps are judged by real track limits (all four wheels beyond the white line), not by touching grass (`e2204df`)
- Imola 4 flying lap in the Maserati: 2:32.9 down to 2:06 (real GT1 pole about 1:46) (`e2204df`)
- The driver watches the wheels while driving: it eases the throttle on wheelspin and the brake on a locking wheel, so top speed and braking are no longer capped (`f570480`)
- Imola 4 laps are 5% faster again (the car accelerates harder out of slow corners); the gap to real GT1 lap times has been measured (`14da41e`)
- Imola 4 laps are about 4% faster: later, harder braking into the chicane and quicker cornering (`b11f3c6`)

### Improvements

- A better steering follower (about 40% less drift from the racing line) and a simulator to tune it; kerb and braking options for chasing faster laps (`243dade`)
- A racing-line optimiser that finds a fast line from any track's width and the car's grip (off by default: the driver cannot follow its lines precisely enough yet) (`5031fd9`)
- A lap-time estimator and several new driving options (live grip, gear choice, edge guard) for chasing real lap times; Imola 4 stays clean at 2:07 (`a9d1307`)
- Shift points are worked out from each car's own engine and gear files (gtr2-shift-points.py) (`f570480`)
- kerb strikes (vertical-dominated jolts or kerb surfaces, up to 45 m/s^2) are counted apart from impacts with a budget of 3 per lap; Imola 4 flying lap 144.5 s to 137.3 s (cornering 11, braking 11, 1 kerb strike) (`7764c40`)
- Laps no longer count the car's own hard braking and cornering as a crash (`77029d0`)

### Documentation

- Imola speed investigation, full sweep results (31 of 34 clean first time, all clean after the fixes); Magny-Cours NT guard settings (`55d5ef2`)
